# Method selection reference

Use methods as composable controls, not mutually exclusive operating systems.

| Signal | Apply | Preserve | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| High uncertainty or costly wrong bet | Lean Build–Measure–Learn | explicit hypothesis, smallest test, actionable metric, pivot/persevere decision | calling a coded feature an MVP without a learning question |
| Ambiguous human problem | Design Thinking / Double Diamond | divergent discovery before convergence, user context, prototypes and iteration | agent-generated personas presented as research |
| Established product with ongoing delivery | Dual-Track Agile | continuous discovery feeding delivery, cross-functional collaboration, small validated opportunities | a detached discovery team throwing specs over a wall |
| Understood software outcome | Scrum | Product Goal, ordered backlog, usable increment, inspection and adaptation | treating a sprint as a fixed-scope mini-waterfall |
| Variable arrival rate or operational work | Kanban | defined workflow, WIP control, active flow management, flow metrics | pushing more work instead of addressing queues |
| Fixed scope, regulation, hardware, irreversible migration | Phase-gated / sequential | baselined requirements, traceability, verification evidence, formal change control | pretending late change is cheap or eliminating feedback loops |

## Default hybrid

1. Discover broadly only enough to frame the opportunity.
2. Use Lean tests on the highest-impact uncertain assumptions.
3. Advance validated opportunities into small vertical delivery slices.
4. Flow slices with Scrum or Kanban according to cadence and arrival pattern.
5. Add formal gates only where cost of change, regulation, contracts, or safety demands them.
6. Keep discovery active while delivery produces new evidence.

## Primary and practitioner references

- Scrum accountabilities, events, artifacts, commitments, empiricism, and usable increments: [The Scrum Guide (Schwaber and Sutherland, 2020)](https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html).
- Kanban definition, workflow, WIP, service-level expectation, and flow metrics: [The Kanban Guide (May 2025)](https://kanbanguides.org/english/).
- Lean experimentation and Build–Measure–Learn: [The Lean Startup methodology](https://theleanstartup.com/principles).
- Human-centred discovery and iterative design: [IDEO Design Thinking](https://designthinking.ideo.com/) and the [Design Council Double Diamond](https://www.designcouncil.org.uk/our-resources/the-double-diamond/).
- Continuous product discovery paired with delivery: [Product Talk, Continuous Discovery](https://www.producttalk.org/2021/08/product-discovery/) and [SVPG, Product Discovery](https://www.svpg.com/product-discovery/).
- Sequential lifecycle controls and technical reviews for high-consequence systems: [NASA Systems Engineering Handbook](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/systems-engineering-handbook/).

Consult the current source when method details materially affect a recommendation. Cite the accessed version and date in user-facing research; do not rely on this summary as a substitute.
